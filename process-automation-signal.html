Process Automation

Digital solutions for process industries.

Process industries demand higher flexibility, consistent quality, and shorter time-to-market — all without expanding headcount. ACS Engitech brings end-to-end electrical automation built on Siemens TIA Portal, deployed across water treatment, chemical, food, and FMCG sectors in Gujarat and beyond.

0 MLDIndia generates around 61,754 MLD of sewage daily — only 37% receives treatment. ACS closes that gap with SCADA-driven electrical automation on Siemens TIA Portal, from influent intake through to ZLD final discharge.

Plant engineers get full process visibility; operators get a straightforward HMI interface they can operate without PLC knowledge.

As a Siemens Authorized System House and Channel Partner since 2014, the team brings direct access to the latest S7-1200/1500 controllers, G120 drives, and WinCC SCADA.

What ACS Delivers

Wastewater treatment automation, engineered end to end.

01

Smart Mimic Design

Siemens WinCC Professional SCADA with plant-layout mimic screens and colour-coded status indicators for each motor and valve.

02

VFD-Based Energy Mgmt

Pumps driven through Siemens G120 drives with auto start/stop sequences based on water demand and emergency triggers.

03

Smart PID Control

Siemens S7-1200/1500 controllers for precise level and flow control — stable process output with fewer manual interventions.

04

Remote SCADA Access

Real-time monitoring and remote setpoint changes from any location over a secure network connection.

05

Data Logs & Reports

Shift-wise logs of critical parameters in SQL Server, with Excel-exportable reports for process analysis and regulatory compliance.

06

Audit Trail & Security

Unique user IDs, password ageing, and complete operator audit trail — meets plant compliance and access control requirements.

07

Third-Party Integration

Modbus TCP/IP, Profinet, and Profibus communication with field instruments — TT, PT, LT, FT and other transmitters.
