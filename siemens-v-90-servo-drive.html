SINAMICS V90 Series

Siemens V90
Servo Drive

SINAMICS V90 is the new member of the SINAMICS drive family, and SIMOTICS S-1FL6 is the new member of the SIMOTICS motor family. Together, they form an optimized servo drive system for positioning, as well as speed and torque control.

Through its optimized design, the system ensures high servo performance in a cost-efficient and easy way as well as a high degree of ruggedness.

SINAMICS V90 single-axis servo drives
Designed to meet general purpose servo applications, taking into consideration the challenges of machine builders and system integrators regarding costs and time to market.

Engineering Tool

SINAMICS V-ASSISTANT

A PC with installed SINAMICS V-ASSISTANT software can be connected to SINAMICS V90 via a standard USB port. Used for setting parameters, test operation, troubleshooting — and has powerful monitoring functions.

  • Setting parameters easily from PC
  • Test operation and commissioning
  • Troubleshooting & diagnostics
  • Powerful monitoring functions
SIMOTICS S-1FL6

Integrated Motor Family

SINAMICS V90 features internal positioning, positioning with pulse train and speed and torque control modes. With integrated real-time auto tuning and automatic suppression of machine resonances, the system automatically optimizes itself to achieve high dynamic performance.

As a result of the high frequency limit up to 1MHz, the pulse train input facilitates high positioning accuracy.
