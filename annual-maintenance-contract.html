Proposal Details

Summary of Proposal

Period of AMC 1 Year, 3 Consecutive Years, 5 Consecutive Years.
Type of AMC Comprehensive type and Non-Comprehensive type.
System Details PLC Panel and SCADA System.
Periodic Maintenance Visit Total 4 visits in a year (once every quarter). Each visit shall comprise of maximum 1 man-day.
Emergency Maintenance Visit Total 2 visits in a year. Each visit comprises of maximum 2 man-days.
E-mail / Telephonic Support Provided by ACS Engitech Pvt. Ltd., Ahmedabad — Monday to Saturday, 10am to 6pm.
Maintenance Checklist

Activities During Preventive Maintenance Visit

  • Checking of loads, logical volume sizes in PLC Panel.
  • Checking of CPU load for all Control Modules and recommending corrective measures if any.
  • Checking Diagnostic logs from system for all nodes.
  • Check for recent list of compile/install for errors and warnings.
  • Check for hardware failures (non-emergency type) reported in the last 03 months.
  • Taking Project Backup and all required backups on Customer provided Pen Drive.
  • Checking of LED indications on various hardware modules of PLC.
  • No Major changes in database shall be carried out; minor changes for problem resolution. No repairs are possible at site.
