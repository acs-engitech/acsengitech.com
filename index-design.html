About ACS Engitech

Reliable automation solutions since 2009

ACS Engitech provides automation and electrical automation solutions — from Machine Automation, Plant Automation and Process Automation to comprehensive Electrical Panel Manufacturing. Established in Ahmedabad with a 36,000 sq. ft. in-house manufacturing facility.

Every panel and control system is engineered around the client's actual process — from control philosophy through commissioning and support, not off-the-shelf templates.

Authorized Siemens Channel Partner since 2014 and Siemens System House since 2018. ISO 9001:2015 certified. CPRI-approved PMCC panels. ERDA IP55 cubicle panels.

Panel supply and commissioning, PLC/HMI/SCADA integration, IEC 61439 and IEC 60204 compliant builds, all delivered from our own 36,000 sq. ft. facility.
