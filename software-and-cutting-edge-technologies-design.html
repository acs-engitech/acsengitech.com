CHAPTER / 02

Our Software Solutions

Expert Software, Exceptional Results

Each solution is built to integrate seamlessly with your existing automation
infrastructure — minimal disruption, maximum insight.

ACS logView — Smart Data Logger
Keep digital records of required parameters with trend views for easy troubleshooting. Digitalize manual entry, collect real-time data, and connect with any PLC/SCADA/Controller. 50 tags inclusive in the basic package.
Energy Monitoring System (EMS)
Monitor key energy parameters and get a full overview of plant consumption through a smart EMS dashboard. Live trends, daily and monthly power consumption, cost prediction, and equipment-wise monitoring.
Siemens Mindsphere IIoT
Connect your plant to the Siemens Mindsphere cloud platform for advanced analytics, predictive maintenance, and real-time remote monitoring. As an authorized Siemens System House, ACS implements Mindsphere end-to-end.
Custom IoT Solutions
Custom IoT solutions that bridge existing automation infrastructure with modern cloud and edge computing. Critical notifications, alarm grouping, alarm dispatch by email — all configurable per your process requirements.
