Panel Manufacturer

Top Electrical Panel
Manufacturers

We have an in-house manufacturing facility of 36000 sq. ft. for Control Panels including LT Panel, PDB Panel, MCC Panel, PLC Panel, VFD Panel, SD/DOL Starter Panel, Servo Panel, Mimic Panel and Instrumentation Panel.

We utilize EPLAN Software as a standard for Power and Control Drawings, linked through ERP for seamless production and minimal errors. Panel fabrication uses CRCA sheets (1.6mm–3mm), finished with a seven-tank pre-treatment and two coats of synthetic enamel paint in RAL 7035 or RAL 7032.

In-house routine tests with Megger (1000V DC) and high voltage tests at 2.5KV for 1 minute are performed before final delivery — ensuring quality and reliability at every step.

IEC Standard

PMCC Panels are designed, assembled and tested according to IEC 61439 and IEC 61641 standard.
