CHAPTER / 01

Intelligent Motor Control

Intelligent MCC *(IMCC) Panel

ACS Engitech manufactures Intelligent Motor Control Centre (IMCC) Panels that integrate smart motor protection and control relays for advanced industrial automation.

IMCC panels go beyond conventional MCC — they provide motor protection, diagnostics, communication and condition monitoring from a single intelligent device at each motor feeder.

Intelligent MCC with Siemens Simocode, Schneider LTMR and ABB UMC 100.3 smart relays for remote monitoring and diagnostics.
Siemens Simocode Pro — advanced motor management system
Schneider LTMR — motor management relay with Modbus
ABB UMC 100.3 — universal motor controller
Remote diagnostics via Profinet, Profibus or Modbus

CHAPTER / 02

Capabilities

IMCC *Advantages

Motor Protection
Comprehensive motor protection including overload, phase failure, thermistor, stall, ground fault and under-voltage protection in a single intelligent relay.
Remote Diagnostics
Motor operating data available over fieldbus — current, voltage, power factor, running hours and fault history accessible from SCADA or PLC systems.
Reduced Wiring
Smart relays reduce hardwired connections between field devices and control panels. A single communication cable replaces multiple signal cables.
Condition Monitoring
Continuous monitoring of motor operating parameters helps identify potential issues before they develop into failures — supporting predictive maintenance.
IEC 61439-1 Compliant
All IMCC panels designed, assembled and tested per IEC 61439-1 standard. CPRI approved with in-house QC testing before final delivery.
Siemens Partnership
As a Siemens Authorized System House since 2018, ACS brings genuine Siemens Simocode expertise and support to every IMCC panel installation.

CHAPTER / 03

Applications

Where IMCC *Panels Are Used

Water Treatment
ETP, STP, ZLD and water supply plants where pump motor status and protection data needs to be visible from the central SCADA system.
Chemical & Process
Mixer, agitator, centrifuge and pump applications where motor condition data supports process quality and production monitoring requirements.
Food & Dairy
CIP systems, conveyors and processing lines where motor status monitoring and smart protection improves process reliability and traceability.
