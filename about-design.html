Our History

A Tradition of Accuracy

From a 3-person team in 2009 to a 90+ strong force with 7000+ installations across industries — here is our journey.

2009

Establishment

Founded in Ahmedabad at SP Ring Road, Odhav Circle. Starting with 3 partners and 1 Employee. Executed first order of Automation panel through OEM.
2012

Export Order

Execute the order of Automation panel through OEM for international clients — marking the first global reach of ACS.
2014

Quality Management System

ISO 9001:2015 Certified Company. Became Authorized Channel Partner of Siemens for PLC & Drives Automation.
2015

Association

Became a distributor of Siemens Smart automation product. Became a dealer of Shavison — the Indian Electronics Company. Shifted new premises, upgraded infrastructure to 6,000 sq.ft. workshop area.
2018

Siemens System House

Became the Authorized System House of Siemens for Automation & Drive. Company registered as ACS Engitech Pvt. Ltd.
2021

CPRI Approval

CPRI Approved the company's PMCC Panel as per IEC standard 61439 after Short Circuit Test.
2026 — Now

Where We Stand Today

More than 90 employees and 7,000+ successful installations of Automation Panels worldwide. Infrastructure upgraded to 36,000 sq.ft.
