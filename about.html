Who we are

We are ACS: Automation and Control System.

Established in 2009 in the industrial city of Ahmedabad, ACS works across machine automation, plant automation and process automation.

Our well-equipped facility handles customised and bulk control panel orders. Every panel uses quality automation products and is manufactured to international standards.

Our history

A tradition of accuracy.

From a three-person team in 2009 to more than 90 people and 7,000+ installations.

  1. 2009

    Establishment

    Founded at SP Ring Road, Odhav Circle, with three partners and one employee. First automation panel order executed through an OEM.

  2. 2012

    Export order

    Automation panels delivered through an OEM to international clients, the first global reach for ACS.

  3. 2014

    Quality management system

    ISO 9001:2015 certification. Authorised Siemens channel partner for PLC and drives automation.

  4. 2015

    Association

    Siemens smart automation distributor and Shavison dealer. New premises with a 6,000 sq. ft. workshop.

  5. 2018

    Siemens System House

    Authorised Siemens System House for automation and drives. Registered as ACS Engitech Pvt. Ltd.

  6. 2021

    CPRI approval

    PMCC panel approved by CPRI to IEC 61439 after short-circuit testing.

  7. Today

    Where we stand

    More than 90 employees, 7,000+ automation panels installed worldwide, and a 36,000 sq. ft. facility.

Capabilities

What we do.

One team from concept to commissioning, and support after handover.

  1. System integration

    PLC, HMI, SCADA, AC and servo motion control drives, process control instruments and sensing devices.

  2. Software and technologies

    IoT solutions, smart data loggers, energy monitoring and data management, Siemens MindSphere for IIoT.

  3. Control panel manufacturing

    Design, manufacture, supply and commissioning of PLC, drive, MCC, SCADA and instrumentation panels.

  4. Project execution

    Concept to commissioning. Design in EPLAN and Siemens TIA Portal; Profinet, Ethernet, Modbus and USS fieldbus.

  5. Custom built for OEMs

    Applications developed and commissioned to the OEM's control philosophy, for long-term association.

  6. Annual maintenance contract

    AMC on Siemens PLC panels and SCADA systems for trouble-free operation of plant and machinery.

  7. Documentation and training

    Electrical drawings, panel test reports, VFD parameter lists and SCADA/HMI operator training at commissioning.
