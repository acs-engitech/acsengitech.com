SIMATIC ET 200SP
Siemens SIMATIC

ET 200SP
Distributed I/O System

The SIMATIC ET 200SP is a scalable, highly flexible I/O system for distributed installation in the control cabinet. Designed with versatility in mind, it connects seamlessly via PROFINET to any SIMATIC controller.

ACS Engitech is an authorized Siemens Channel Partner since 2014 — offering the complete ET 200SP module range for your automation projects.

Interface Modules (IM) — CPU-integrated or standalone
Digital Input / Output modules up to 16 channels
Analog Input / Output modules with high resolution
Technology & Communication modules
BaseUnits — self-coding, tool-free wiring
Wide temperature range — suitable for harsh environments
