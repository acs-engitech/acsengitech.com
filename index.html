About ACS Engitech

Reliable Automation Solutions Since 2009

ACS Engitech provides automation and electrical automation solutions - from Machine Automation, Plant Automation and Process Automation to comprehensive Electrical Panel Manufacturing. Established in Ahmedabad with a 36,000 sq. ft. in-house manufacturing facility.

Authorized Siemens Channel Partner since 2014 and Siemens System House since 2018. ISO 9001:2015 certified. CPRI approved PMCC Panels. ERDA IP55 cubicle panels. Committed to safe industrial solutions for factories across Gujarat.

Our Services

End-to-End Automation, Every Industry

From process control to IIoT-based data solutions, ACS combines a full suite of automation services for demanding industrial applications - concept to commissioning.

Panel Manufacturing
PLC, MCC, VFD, PCC, Servo, Mimic and Instrumentation Panels - EPLAN designed, IEC 61439 compliant.
System Integration
PLC, HMI, SCADA, Motion Control Drives (AC/Servo), Process Control Instruments - end-to-end turnkey solutions.
Process Automation
Water/Waste Water, Chemical, Food & Dairy, FMCG - digital process automation with web-based graphical interface.
Machine Automation
VFD and Servo based motion control for Saw Pipe, Steel, Packaging, Plastic & Rubber, Material Handling industries.
Documentation & Training
Electrical Drawings, Panel Test Reports, VFD parameter lists and SCADA/HMI operation training at commissioning stage.
IIoT & Smart Solutions
IoT solutions · Smart Data Logger (ACS LogView) · Energy Monitoring · Data Management System.
AMC - Annual Maintenance
Annual Maintenance Contract for Siemens PLC Panels and SCADA. 4 preventive visits/year, 10×7 break-fix support.
Custom OEM Solutions
Develop and commission customized applications as per Control Philosophy provided by OEMs for long-term association.
