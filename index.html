03 / Inside the panel

Open the door.
See the engineering.

Every component has a job. In a PLC panel, protection, control power, logic and field wiring are laid out, ferruled and labelled for safe operation and fast maintenance.

  1. Isolator & 24 V DC supplies Main switch and clean control power
  2. MCB distribution Protection for every circuit
  3. PLC CPU & I/O modules Siemens PLC, programmed in TIA Portal
  4. Industrial Ethernet switch PLC, HMI & SCADA network
  5. Interface relays Isolated outputs to the field
  6. Signal isolators Clean analog signals from instruments
  7. Terminal blocks & ferruled wiring Labelled, traceable connections
  8. Cable ducts Segregated power & control routing
