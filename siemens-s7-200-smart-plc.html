Siemens S7-200 Smart PLC

The Siemens S7-200 Smart PLC is a compact, high-performance programmable logic controller built for diverse industrial automation applications. With integrated Ethernet, high-speed I/O, and a wide range of expansion modules, it handles everything from simple machine control to complex multi-axis coordination.

ACS Engitech is an authorized Siemens Channel Partner since 2014. We supply, program, and commission S7-200 Smart systems across process, machine, and plant automation projects.
