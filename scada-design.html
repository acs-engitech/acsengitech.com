SCADA

ACS builds connected data systems for SCADA reporting, energy monitoring, smart logging, IoT dashboards, and automation visibility.

CHAPTER / 01

System Integration

*SCADA Systems

ACS Engitech understands the importance of process visibility. Getting accurate data in a timely manner is critical to manage automated systems.

Our SCADA systems provide advanced visualization and data monitoring from multiple systems and components, designed and implemented on the Siemens platform using TIA Portal.

We use industry-standard open protocols including Modbus, Profibus, Profinet and OPC. Our designs are compatible with most web browsers and mobile devices — enabling true turnkey solutions.
Siemens WinCC Professional / Advance SCADA platform
MOVICON SCADA for process visualization
Web-based remote monitoring from any device
Multi-vendor system integration support

CHAPTER / 02

Capabilities

SCADA *Features

Graphics & Animations
Design of process mimic screens with excellent graphics, animations and dynamic elements — making plant operations easy to monitor at a glance.
Alarm Management
Comprehensive alarm system with buzzer, email and SMS notifications. Alarms stored with date, time and sequential order of appearance.
Data & Event Logging
Complete data and event logging with secure SQL Server storage. All process parameters recorded for traceability and compliance reporting.
Real Time & Historical Trends
Live trends and historical trend analysis of analogue parameters from field instruments including TT, PT, LT and FT sensors.
Recipe Management
Full recipe management capability for batch processes — store, recall and manage production recipes with version control.
Report Generation
Generate shift-wise, daily and monthly reports in Excel format. Summary reports for process analysis and management review.
Multi-Level Protection
Unique User IDs and passwords with password-ageing facility. Audit trail for all operator actions and parameter changes.
OPC & I/O Connectivity
OPC and I/O drive connectivity supporting Modbus, Profibus, Profinet and Modbus TCP/IP for third-party device communication.

CHAPTER / 03

Applications

SCADA for Every *Industry

ACS has experience designing and implementing SCADA systems for a wide range of process and discrete manufacturing industries.

Water & Waste Water
Smart PID control, automated startup/shutdown, ETP, STP, ZLD and UF RO plant monitoring with remote SCADA connectivity.
Chemical & Pharma
Batch process monitoring, distillation and fermentation plant control, recipe management and regulatory compliance reporting.
Food & Dairy
CIP system automation, pasteurizer control, centralized plant monitoring and production reporting for food processing facilities.
Energy Monitoring
Real-time energy dashboards, machine-wise consumption tracking, daily and monthly reports and cost prediction analysis.
Steel & Manufacturing
Centralized monitoring of rolling mills, material handling, furnace control and production data management systems.
Remote Monitoring
Remote SCADA connectivity from anywhere — monitor plant parameters, respond to alarms and track production from any location.
