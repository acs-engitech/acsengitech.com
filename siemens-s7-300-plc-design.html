CHAPTER / 01

SIMATIC S7-300 Series

Siemens *S7-300 PLC

The SIMATIC S7-300 is the modular mini controller for low to medium performance requirements, providing a wide range of modules that can be combined as needed.

It offers a high degree of flexibility and user-friendliness with a variety of CPUs and a comprehensive selection of modules. Designed for space-saving installation in the control cabinet.

CHAPTER / 02

Applications

Where *S7-300 Excels

The S7-300 is the proven workhorse for standard automation tasks across Gujarat's manufacturing sector.

Manufacturing Plants
Ideal for factory automation tasks requiring moderate to high I/O count with reliable, proven technology. Used in textile, plastic & rubber, and FMCG industries.
Process Industries
Water treatment, chemical processing, food & dairy — the S7-300 handles continuous process control with stable performance and a long product lifecycle.
Machine Builders (OEMs)
Compact modular design makes it perfect for panel-mounting in OEM machines where space and cost are key considerations. Preferred by machine builders across Gujarat.
